# Detailed director-level package

Use this only when the creator asks for a full package, a storyboard, or expanded production detail.

## Full package layout

1. **Angle and audience**: one short paragraph on who this is for and why this angle works.
2. **Hook options**: three hooks, each with its approach (curiosity, demonstration, benefit, and so on), a one-line strength note, and a clear recommendation.
3. **Script**: the complete Kuwaiti Arabic voiceover in one clean block, with the estimated duration.
4. **Captions**: three styles, each labeled:
   - SHORT: concise and memorable.
   - PROFESSIONAL: informative and polished.
   - ENGAGEMENT: built to invite relevant discussion.
   Then one line recommending the strongest.
5. **Storyboard**: see format below.
6. **Edit plan**: first two seconds, on-screen text list, B-roll and screen recording points, cuts and zooms, emphasis words, sound effects, ending.
7. **CTA options**: two or three, each labeled, with one recommended.
8. **Quality score**: total and the six rubric scores with a short reason each.

## Storyboard format

Use a table with these columns:

| Timestamp | Voiceover (Kuwaiti Arabic) | On-screen text | Visual | Camera or animation | Editing and sound |

Rules:
- Timestamps run continuously and match the script's pacing.
- Each row covers one idea or one shot, usually 2 to 6 seconds.
- Visuals describe real footage or screen recordings the creator can capture.
- Camera or animation notes stay simple: static, slow push-in, quick zoom on a button, cut.
- Sound notes list only purposeful effects, such as a click on a toggle or a soft whoosh on a transition.

## Tool-specific video prompt

When the creator asks for a prompt for a video generation or editing tool, include: the subject, the shot list in order, camera movement, lighting and color mood, pacing, on-screen text if any, audio notes, aspect ratio (9:16 for Reels), and duration. Keep the prompt in English unless the tool requires otherwise.
