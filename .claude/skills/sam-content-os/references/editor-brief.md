# Editor brief

A one-page handoff for a video editor, based on a finished package. It lets the editor work without asking follow-up questions.

## When to use

Use this when the creator asks for a brief for an editor, a freelancer, or a team member, or says they will not edit the video themselves.

## Format

Use these sections in this order, in clear Arabic:

1. **الهدف:** one sentence on what the video should achieve and who it is for.
2. **المدة والنسبة:** target length in seconds, aspect ratio 9:16, and the maximum allowed length.
3. **الترتيب:** the order of shots and screen recordings, one line each, matching the voiceover timing.
4. **النص على الشاشة:** the exact on-screen text, with the moment each one appears.
5. **التسجيلات المطلوبة:** the screen recordings or footage the editor will receive, and any that must be captured again.
6. **القص والتكبير:** where cuts happen, where zooms are used, and the rule that effects appear only where they help understanding.
7. **الصوت:** voiceover file to use, background music (if any, and its volume relative to the voice), and the sound effects listed by moment.
8. **الترجمة المكتوبة:** the timed caption list, if the video is 30 seconds or longer.
9. **النهاية:** the last frame, the CTA text, and the end card if used.
10. **ملاحظات ممنوعة:** things to avoid, such as stock footage, unnecessary transitions, or claims not in the script.

## Rules

- Do not add new information that is not in the approved script.
- Keep each section short. The whole brief should fit on one page.
- If the creator has not provided a voiceover file, say so in the brief and mark it as pending.
