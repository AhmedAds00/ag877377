# On-screen captions (subtitles for the video)

Many viewers watch Reels with the sound off. Timed on-screen captions help them follow the script, and they also help with the retention of the first seconds.

## When to include

Include this block when the video is 30 seconds or longer. For shorter videos, a few key words on screen is enough, and the editing guide already covers that.

## Format

Break the voiceover into lines of 3 to 7 words, each shown for about 1.5 to 3 seconds. Keep one idea per line. Use the same wording as the spoken script, so the captions match what is said.

Present it as a timed list:

```
00:00 - 00:02  [line text]
00:02 - 00:04  [line text]
```

## Rules

- Keep line breaks natural for Arabic reading. Do not split a phrase in the middle of a verb or a name.
- Highlight at most one word per line, by suggesting its color or size in the editing guide, not in the caption text.
- For English terms inside Arabic lines (Plan Mode, iPhone, shortcut), keep the English spelling exactly.
- Total caption lines should match the spoken word count within a small margin.

## Where it goes

Place this block after the editing guide, as a separate section titled "الترجمة المكتوبة على الشاشة". Do not add it to the voiceover block.
